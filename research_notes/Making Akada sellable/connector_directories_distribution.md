## Key question 1: Claude connector directory — how to get listed, review, verification, categories, size, surfacing, plan tiers, public usage data

### Takeaway
As of September 2026 the Claude directory is open to anyone on a paid Claude plan, lists ~867 connectors across 16 categories (one of which is "Education"), auto-lists submissions as "Community" after an automated policy scan, escalates to "Verified" only by Anthropic's own automatic assessment, ranks by usage, and is reachable by Free users (directory connectors are available on all plans; Free users can also add one custom connector). There is no public install data; only the developer's own dashboard.

### Cited Findings

Timeline and size
- Anthropic launched "Integrations" (remote MCP servers in Claude) on May 1, 2025 in beta for Max, Team and Enterprise with 10 launch partners (Atlassian, Zapier, Cloudflare, Intercom, Asana, Square, Sentry, PayPal, Linear, Plaid); an update dated June 3, 2025 extended access to Pro. — [Claude blog: Integrations](https://claude.com/blog/integrations)
- The Connectors Directory launched July 14, 2025 with partners Notion, Canva, Stripe, Figma, Socket and Prisma; at launch, remote app/service connectors were "available to paid plan users only". — [Claude blog: Connectors directory](https://claude.com/blog/connectors-directory)
- On April 23, 2026 Anthropic announced consumer connectors (AllTrails, Audible, Booking.com, Instacart, Credit Karma, TurboTax, Resy, Spotify, StubHub, Taskrabbit, Thumbtack, Tripadvisor, Uber, Uber Eats, Viator), said the directory "has grown to over 200 connectors" since July 2025, and stated "Connectors are available on all plans, with mobile in beta." — [Claude blog: New connectors for everyday life](https://claude.com/blog/connectors-for-everyday-life)
- The public marketplace page (fetched Sep 29, 2026) shows "867 connectors" and lists these categories: Commerce & shopping, Communication, Consumer health, Creative, Data & analytics, Developer tools, Education, Financial services, Health & life sciences, Legal, Media & entertainment, Nonprofit, Other, Productivity, Sales & marketing, Travel. Featured sections: Top connectors, Top plugins, Trending connectors (6), New connectors (5). — [claude.com/connectors](https://claude.com/connectors)
- The Claude Marketplace (announced Sept 23, 2026) says "2,000+ connectors and plugins" are available; agents/products from partners can be bought with "a portion of their committed Anthropic spend" — no revenue share or paid placement is disclosed. — [Claude blog: Claude Marketplace](https://claude.com/blog/claude-marketplace)
- [snippet only] A third-party count claimed 369 entries across 11 categories on June 19, 2026, "up from around 50 at the start of the year". — [DirJournal](https://www.dirjournal.com/blogs/claude-connectors-list)

Who can submit and how
- "Anyone on a paid Claude plan can submit, there's no partner program to apply to first, and Anthropic checks each submission before it's listed." Plan: "Pro, Max, Team, or Enterprise. Free accounts can't submit." On Pro/Max you submit from your own account; on Team/Enterprise an Owner submits. — [Publish to the directory](https://claude.com/docs/directory/publish)
- The developer portal (claude.ai/directory/manage) opened with the Sept 25, 2026 "Build plugins for Claude" post: "The directory submission portal is open to developers on paid Claude plans." Portal features cited: auto-validation with safety scanning, review status, "installs by product surface and version", and "how often your listing is viewed and which searches lead people to it". — [Claude blog: Build plugins for Claude](https://claude.com/blog/build-plugins-for-claude)
- Before the portal, submissions required a Team/Enterprise org: a Sept 14, 2026 PR preparing a real submission notes the process "reaches human-only gates requiring a Team/Enterprise organization, portal submission, and reviewer credentials at Anthropic", and lists the requirements as "HTTPS Streamable HTTP, OAuth, tool title plus readOnlyHint or destructiveHint, short tool names, separate read vs write tools, public docs, a privacy URL, and listing copy." — [kentcdodds/kody PR #2324](https://github.com/kentcdodds/kody/pull/2324); [snippet only] same Team/Enterprise requirement stated Aug 2026 by [sunpeak](https://sunpeak.ai/blogs/claude-connector-directory-submission/)
- Pre-submission checklist: server remote over HTTPS; "OAuth 2.0 if your tools act on a user's account, or no authentication for public data"; "You don't need Verified status or any separate approval for authentication to work"; every tool has a `title` and `readOnlyHint`/`destructiveHint`; tested as a custom connector; documentation URL, privacy policy URL, support contact, icon; test account "for a fully populated account"; carousel screenshots (PNG, ≥1000px wide, 3–5 images) only if it is an MCP App. Listing fields: name ≤100 chars, one-liner ≤200, description ≤2,000, "one to five categories", permanent URL slug. Seven policy acknowledgments (directory guidelines, first-party API usage, financial transactions, AI media generation, prompt injection, conversation data collection, public documentation). Local/desktop-extension (MCPB) listings are deprecated. — [Submit a connector](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/submission)
- Authentication types: `oauth_dcr` and `oauth_cimd` "Supported by default"; `oauth_anthropic_creds` and `custom_connection` require emailing mcp-review@anthropic.com; `none` supported. PKCE S256 required; callback `https://claude.ai/api/mcp/auth_callback`; OAuth endpoints must respond within 10 s. "For servers expecting high traffic from the directory, prefer CIMD or oauth_anthropic_creds over DCR. DCR causes Claude to register a new client on every fresh connection." — [Authentication for connectors](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/authentication)
- Lazy ("mixed") auth is supported: public tools can be called before sign-in and an inline Connect card appears only when a protected tool is called. — [Lazy authentication](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/lazy-authentication)

Review and verification
- "Anthropic scans your submission automatically for policy compliance and, by default, lists it as a Community connector with no action from you. Some submissions also get a review from a person, and those review times vary with queue volume." Escalation: "Anthropic may then escalate listings flagged as highly useful to Claude users to Verified review, which is higher touch and slower... That escalation is assessed automatically. There's no separate application for the Verified label." — [Submit a connector](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/submission); [Connector verification](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/verification)
- Label meanings: Verified = "Anthropic has tested a Verified connector's tools for quality and compatibility"; Community = "Anthropic screens Community connectors before listing them but hasn't reviewed them in depth", and "Before you connect one, Claude shows a reminder that it hasn't been reviewed in depth." Labels affect display/discovery, not capabilities. — [Connector verification](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/verification)
- Review criteria (most common rejection reasons): catch-all `api_request` tools with a `method` parameter are rejected; read and write must be separate tools; tool names ≤64 chars; descriptions must not contain prompt-injection patterns; "Every tool must return a successful response when called with valid parameters"; "Your server must call your own first-party APIs... The MCP server domain should match your service"; not accepted: money/crypto transfer, AI image/video/audio generation. — [Connector pre-submission checklist](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/review-criteria)
- Statuses: Draft, In review, Changes requested, Not approved, Approved (you must click Publish), Published. "Review time isn't fixed." — [Track your directory submission](https://claude.com/docs/directory/submission-status)
- Plugin submissions are limited to 10 per organization per 24 hours; the plugin Data-handling step asks "whether it's intended for people under 18". — [Submit your plugin](https://claude.com/docs/plugins/submit)

Surfacing and ranking
- "The same catalog serves claude.ai, Cowork, Desktop, mobile, and Claude Code"; "Directory connectors are eligible for Suggested Connectors, which are in-chat recommendations shown when relevant to the user's task. Every directory entry is included automatically"; "Ranking is usage-based, similar to other app stores"; no DNS/.well-known proof required (that is only for the open MCP Registry). — [Connectors directory](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/directory)
- "Only a directory listing makes your connector discoverable inside Claude. Listings elsewhere, including the open MCP Registry, don't." Custom connectors "are never suggested". A directory listing gets a permanent URL `https://claude.ai/directory/connectors/SLUG`; unlisted servers can still be distributed with a prefilled install link `https://claude.ai/customize/connectors?modal=add-custom-connector&connectorName=...&connectorUrl=...`. — [Directory vs custom connectors](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/directory-vs-custom)
- Users browse "by category or scroll through the complete list" from the "+" menu or Customize > Connectors. — [Help Center: Use connectors](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11176164-use-connectors-to-extend-claude-s-capabilities)

Plan tiers for end users
- "Web connectors are available for all users on Claude, Cowork, Claude Desktop, and Claude Mobile (iOS and Android)." "Custom connectors using remote MCP are available on Claude, Cowork, and Claude Desktop for users on free, Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise plans. Free users are limited to one custom connector." — [Help Center: Use connectors](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11176164-use-connectors-to-extend-claude-s-capabilities); confirmed in the Aug 11, 2026 article [Get started with custom connectors](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11175166-get-started-with-custom-connectors-using-remote-mcp)
- On Team plans, members without permission see a Request button; Owners enable connectors org-wide. — [Connectors directory](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/directory)

Usage data available to a publisher (not public)
- Metrics "are in beta", computed daily, rows with <5 calls omitted. Cards: Directory rank ("your position among published directory servers... ranked by the number of distinct Claude accounts that sent your server any MCP message in the last 30 days"), a "Trending" tag for "servers in the top 10 by recent growth", Used a tool (distinct accounts with ≥1 `tools/call` in 30 days), Tool calls (30d), Error rate (30d), per-product and per-tool tables. Health badge: Healthy ≤2% errors, Degraded >5%. Only Claude-surface traffic is counted. — [Manage your directory listing](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/managing-your-listing)
- A published plugin's Usage tab adds "installs, active accounts, and retention", a "Directory funnel: listing views, install clicks, and installs", up to 90 days, CSV export. — [After publishing](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/after-publishing)

### Inferences
- The gate to entry has dropped sharply in the last month (Team/Enterprise org → any paid plan, portal instead of a form), so "being listed" is now table stakes rather than a differentiator; the 867-listing count in September vs "over 200" in April implies roughly 4x growth in five months.
- Because ranking is usage-based and Suggested Connectors are automatic, distribution inside the directory compounds existing usage rather than creating it: a new Community listing with few connections will sit below Verified, high-usage connectors.
- Akada (OAuth, ~41 tools) already meets the technical bar; the practical work is titles/annotations on all 41 tools, read/write separation, ≤64-char names, a privacy policy, public docs, and a populated test account. The 41-tool count is not itself a documented rejection reason, but per-tool functional testing under Verified review scales with tool count.
- Free-plan reach is real: a Free Claude user can connect Akada from the directory (directory connectors are on all plans) even though they can add only one custom connector.

### Gaps
- No public, per-connector install or usage figures exist anywhere for the Claude directory; only the developer's private dashboard. No Anthropic statement of total directory installs or connector-driven signups was found.
- Could not retrieve the actual Education-category contents (the category filter is client-side; the fetch returned the unfiltered list).
- The exact wording of the seven policy acknowledgments (e.g., what "financial transactions" acknowledgment requires) was not retrievable beyond their titles.

Apps in ChatGPT are MCP servers plus a UI component; submissions are open via the OpenAI developer platform and reviewed against public guidelines, but as of the latest docs found, selling "digital goods, subscriptions, or in-app services isn't yet allowed", in-app checkout is limited to select physical-goods marketplaces in beta, and no third-party results data was found. All OpenAI pages were blocked in this session, so every item below is snippet-only.

### Inferences
- Akada's existing MCP server is reusable as the backend of a ChatGPT app; the extra work is the UI component and OpenAI's review, not a new integration.
- For a subscription study planner, ChatGPT offers no sanctioned in-chat monetization path as of the latest docs found: subscriptions must be sold on Akada's own site, and the app can only act as a funnel.
- ChatGPT's user base (secondary statistics sites report ~900 M weekly active users and a 53.9% share of AI-chatbot web traffic in May 2026 vs 9.2% for Claude — [SQ Magazine](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/chatgpt-statistics/), snippet only) dwarfs Claude's, but the guideline that apps must offer workflows "not natively supported by ChatGPT" is a review risk for a planner whose core value (scheduling, reminders, study plans) ChatGPT can partly do natively.

### Gaps
- No quantitative results for any third-party ChatGPT app (installs, referrals, conversions) were found; coverage found was headlines only ("Apps in ChatGPT: Higher Conversions or Lost Users?" — ninetwothree.co; "Retailers are rushing to build AI apps, it's unclear if shoppers will use them" — theatdb.com), neither readable in-session.

- The "migrated to the plugin directory" claim (July 9, 2026) is unverified and may indicate a rename of "apps" in ChatGPT; needs checking before it is repeated.

## Key question 4: The open MCP Registry and third-party directories (Smithery, Glama, mcp.so, PulseMCP) — consumer users or developers only?

### Takeaway
The official registry is a metadata-only, developer-facing index that downstream aggregators scrape; Anthropic states explicitly that publishing there does not surface a server in Claude. Nothing found suggests these directories deliver consumer users.

### Cited Findings
- The registry "provides MCP clients with a list of MCP servers, like an app store for MCP servers"; preview launched September 2025 with a warning that "breaking changes or data resets may occur"; API freeze (v0.1) October 2025; publishing requires namespace proof via GitHub OAuth/OIDC, DNS or HTTP verification; it stores metadata only and has no end-user browsing UI. — [modelcontextprotocol/registry README](https://github.com/modelcontextprotocol/registry)
- Ecosystem vision: "Server creators publish once, and all consumers (MCP clients, aggregators, marketplaces) reference the same canonical data"; "subregistries add value... by providing curation, or extending it with additional metadata"; "MCP registries are metaregistries" hosting metadata only; backed by "Anthropic, GitHub, PulseMCP and Microsoft". — [ecosystem-vision.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/modelcontextprotocol/registry/main/docs/design/ecosystem-vision.md)
- Aggregator guide: "The MCP Registry provides an unauthenticated read-only REST API that aggregators can use to populate their data stores"; aggregators "should scrape data on a regular but infrequent basis (e.g., once per hour)"; a "deleted" status "typically indicates that a server has violated our permissive moderation policy". — [registry-aggregators.mdx](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/modelcontextprotocol/registry/main/docs/modelcontextprotocol-io/registry-aggregators.mdx)
- Latest releases: v1.8.1 (Aug 6, 2026), v1.8.0 (Jul 13, 2026), v1.7.9 (May 12, 2026); release notes mention no "general availability" milestone. — [registry releases](https://github.com/modelcontextprotocol/registry/releases)
- [snippet only] The Sept 8, 2025 announcement described the registry as an "open catalog and API for discovering publicly available MCP servers" in preview with GA "later". — [MCP blog: Introducing the MCP Registry](https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2025-09-08-mcp-registry-preview/)
- Anthropic: "The Anthropic Directory is independent of the open MCP Registry and the modelcontextprotocol/servers GitHub repository. Publishing to those doesn't surface your server in Claude." — [Directory vs custom](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/directory-vs-custom)

### Inferences
- Registry listing is worth doing for hygiene (it feeds Smithery/Glama/PulseMCP-style aggregators and Claude Code/IDE users), but its audience is developers configuring clients, not students choosing a study app.

### Gaps
- Smithery, Glama, mcp.so and PulseMCP pages were all blocked; no listing counts, traffic figures, or evidence of consumer referrals from them could be gathered.

## Key question 6: Platform terms — can a third-party connector charge users or require a paid account? Revenue share or verified programs?

### Takeaway
Anthropic charges no fee and takes no revenue share, and its policy is silent on requiring a paid account with your own service (the submission form explicitly asks what "accounts, plans, or other setup" users need), but bans in-connector money transfer and advertising; OpenAI currently forbids selling subscriptions/digital goods inside ChatGPT and has not published a revenue share. Neither platform offers an application-based "verified" program.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- Akada can lawfully gate tools behind an Akada account and a paid Akada plan on Claude, provided payment happens on Akada's own site and the connector itself never moves money; lazy auth lets some tools (e.g., generic study-technique lookups) work before sign-in as a funnel.
- The "financial transactions" acknowledgment plus the ban on money transfer means an "upgrade to Pro" tool that charges a card inside Claude is not allowed; an `ui/open-link` to Akada's checkout page (allow-listed origin) is the compliant pattern.
- Anthropic's unilateral removal right and usage-based ranking mean a listing is a licence, not an asset.

### Gaps
- Whether Anthropic reviewers reject connectors that are unusable without a paid third-party plan was not documented either way; the Verified listings observed only require sign-in.
- OpenAI's current stance on apps that require a paid account at sign-in (as opposed to selling in-chat) could not be read directly.

## Key question 8: How defensible is being early? Lessons from ChatGPT plugins, GPT Store, Alexa skills, Slack App Directory

### Takeaway
Every prior "apps inside an assistant" store rewarded early entrants with press, not durable distribution: ChatGPT plugins were deprecated 13 months after launch, the GPT Store's revenue program stayed an invite-only US pilot, Alexa's monetization programs repeatedly changed under developers, and Slack needed an $80 M fund to keep developers. Structurally, a Claude listing is non-exclusive, removable at will, usage-ranked, and technologically identical to a URL any user can paste.

### Cited Findings
- [snippet only] ChatGPT Plugins: announced March 2023; "new conversations disabled on March 19, 2024, and plugin conversations stopped working on April 9, 2024"; developers "felt abandoned, making them hesitant to invest in GPTs"; OpenAI emailed plugin developers to build GPTs instead. — [The AI Cemetery](https://theaicemetery.com/chatgpt-plugins/); [Analytics India Magazine](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-features/chatgpt-plugins-are-dead-and-developers-are-not-happy/); [Tom Guider](https://tomguider.com/chatgpt-plugins-vs-gpts/)
- [snippet only] GPT Store: "over 3 million GPT apps already created"; revenue program announced for Q1 2024, US-only, engagement-based; later described as "reserved for a tiny number of GPT creators in an invite-only pilot program in the US". — [App Developer Magazine](https://appdevelopermagazine.com/gpt-app-store-revenue-and-api-insights/); [WildnetEdge](https://www.wildnetedge.com/?p=22872). A claim that "top creators [earn] six figures annually" comes from a promotional source and is unverified. — [Communipass](https://communipass.com/blog/how-to-monetize-chatgpt-2026/)
- [snippet only] Alexa skills: April 2017 policy banned ads in skills; June 2017 the first skill ad network (VoiceLabs) shut down as a result; May 2017 Amazon started direct "developer rewards" payments, expanded in Aug 2017 to categories including education; Oct 2017 skill subscriptions; Dec 2021 Amazon cut its commission from 30% to 20% for developers under $1 M; an Alexa developer Slack's running joke was a snow-cone emoji because "it's easier to make more money selling snow cones than building Alexa skills"; "numerous efforts to 'monetize' the Alexa operation failed". — TechCrunch: [ad ban](https://techcrunch.com/2017/04/20/amazons-new-alexa-developer-policy-now-bans-all-ads-except-in-music-and-flash-briefings/), [VoiceLabs shutdown](https://techcrunch.com/2017/06/15/the-first-ad-network-for-alexa-skills-shuts-down-following-amazons-policy-changes/), [developer rewards](https://techcrunch.com/2017/05/16/amazon-begins-rewarding-top-performing-alexa-skill-developers-with-direct-payments/), [rewards expansion](https://techcrunch.com/2017/08/16/amazon-expands-program-that-pays-alexa-developers-for-top-performing-voice-apps), [subscriptions](https://techcrunch.com/2017/10/25/amazon-introduces-subscriptions-for-alexa-skills-makes-them-free-for-prime-members/), [commission cut](https://techcrunch.com/2021/12/15/amazon-to-lower-its-cut-of-alexa-skill-developer-revenue-starting-in-2022); [Opus Research, Dec 2022](https://opusresearch.net/wordpress/2022/12/01/amazon-reinventing-alexa-it-doesnt-look-like-it/)
- [snippet only] Slack launched its App Directory in December 2015 alongside an $80 M fund with six VCs to attract "Slack-first" developers; the open question then was "whether developers could find their own viable business models on the Slack platform". — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=1252126); [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/apps/2015/12/16/slack-introduces-80m-developer-fund-and-extensive-app-directory/)
- Structural facts about Claude's directory (primary): "Ranking is usage-based, similar to other app stores" — [Connectors directory](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/directory); "Connectors in the directory and custom connectors you add yourself use the same technology, so if you have a connector's URL, you can add it as a custom connector whether or not it's in the directory" — [Connector verification](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/verification); Anthropic "may remove or refuse to display any Software... at any time for any reason" — [Directory Terms](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13145338-anthropic-software-directory-terms); the URL slug is permanent — [After publishing](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/after-publishing)
- Portability cuts both ways: the MCP Apps SDK provides tooling to "Migrate from the OpenAI Apps SDK", and one server serves Claude, ChatGPT, VS Code and Goose. — [Get started with MCP Apps](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/mcp-apps/getting-started); [ext-apps README](https://github.com/modelcontextprotocol/ext-apps)
- Anthropic deprecated its own desktop-extension (MCPB) listings within ~14 months of introducing them: "Desktop extension listings in the directory are deprecated, and the directory no longer accepts local MCP servers packaged as desktop extensions." — [Publish to the directory](https://claude.com/docs/directory/publish)

### Inferences
- "The MCP part is the moat" is not supported by any platform mechanism: the protocol is open, listing is non-exclusive and free, and Notion/Todoist/Google Calendar (Verified, high usage) already own the ranking slots a study planner competes for. Any moat has to come from Akada's data model and retention, with the connector as a distribution surface.
- Early-mover value in these stores has historically been (a) launch-partner PR and (b) a head start on usage-based ranking; the second is real on Claude because rank is computed on 30-day distinct accounts, so a listing that accrues users early stays visible — but only while usage persists.
- The greatest risk is not competition but platform churn: plugins (2023–24), MCPB listings (2025–26) and Alexa's monetization rules all changed under developers within 12–18 months.

### Gaps
- Could not read any of the plugin/Alexa/Slack retrospectives in full; numbers above come from search summaries of the linked articles.
- No documented case of a small developer whose Claude/ChatGPT listing was delisted or overtaken, so the "loser" side of first-mover history for these two stores is undocumented.

## Key question 9 (cross-cutting): What specifically affects a solo developer in Pakistan

### Takeaway
