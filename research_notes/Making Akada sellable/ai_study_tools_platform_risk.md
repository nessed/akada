## KQ1. What OpenAI has shipped for students by Sept 2026 (Study Mode, ChatGPT Edu, student promos, memory, Projects, Apps SDK)

### Takeaway
OpenAI's student stack is a tutoring behaviour (Study Mode), free-text memory (with the 2026 "Dreaming" and "Memory Sources" upgrades), file-and-instruction Projects with project-scoped memory, institutional ChatGPT Edu, seasonal free-Plus promos, and (Aug 2026) an Edu-only "college student" add-on that turns materials into study guides/quizzes/flashcards. None of it is a semester model: no courses-as-objects, no deadline ledger, no grade math, no hours log.

### Cited Findings
- Study Mode launched 29 July 2025; it "helps you work through problems step by step instead of just getting an answer", uses Socratic questioning, hints, scaffolded responses, quizzes, and "lessons are tailored to your skill level, which is evaluated through the review of stored memories from previous chats" — [OpenAI, Introducing study mode](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-study-mode/) (via search summary); launch date corroborated by [TechCrunch, 29 Jul 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/29/openai-launches-study-mode-in-chatgpt).
- As of 2026 Study Mode "is available across ChatGPT plans globally on web, iOS, and Android, and is available for all ChatGPT models" — [OpenAI Help Center, Using study mode in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11780217-chatgpt-study-mode-faq) (via search summary); a secondary explainer describes the same scope plus memory-based personalisation — [Appscribed, ChatGPT Study Mode in 2026](https://appscribed.com/chatgpt-study-mode/).
- ChatGPT Edu institutional rollouts to students continued through 2026: USC opened its ChatGPT Edu workspace to students, faculty and staff for Spring 2026 (13 Jan 2026) — [USC, We Are SC](https://we-are.usc.edu/2026/01/13/usc-launches-chatgpt-edu-for-students-faculty-and-staff/); UCCS launched the student-access phase on 14 Aug 2026 for all full- and part-time students — [UCCS News, 14 Aug 2026](https://news.uccs.edu/2026/08/14/chatgpt-edu-launches-for-students/).
- 2026 Back to School offer: "four free monthly billing periods of ChatGPT Plus" for current full-/part-time college and graduate students at eligible US degree-granting institutions, claim by 31 Oct 2026 — [OpenAI Help Center, ChatGPT Back to School offer](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001493-chatgpt-back-to-school-offer-for-students) (via search summary); landing page [chatgpt.com/students/2026](https://chatgpt.com/students/2026/).
- 4 Aug 2026: OpenAI announced "a trio of new education-focused offerings: one for K-12 teachers, another for college educators, and a third for college students"; the college-student offering "allows students to create study guides, practice quizzes, flashcards, and interactive explanations using the materials they're already studying" and the three are "available through ChatGPT Edu and ChatGPT for Teachers district deployments" — [The Register, 4 Aug 2026](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/08/04/openai-wants-teachers-and-profs-to-foist-their-work-off-on-chatgpt/5283064) (via search summary; the summary calls them "plugins" — exact product name unverified).
- OpenAI marketing programmes: "ChatGPT Futures: Class of 2026 — 26 student innovators" (positioning the class of 2026 as "the first generation to start and finish college with ChatGPT") — [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-futures-class-of-2026/) (via search summary); Student Collective 2026–27 campus leads receive a ChatGPT subscription, Codex credits, event funding and a cash stipend — [OpenAI Student Collective](https://openai.com/student-collective/) and [Opportunities for Youth, 31 Jul 2026](https://opportunitiesforyouth.org/2026/07/31/openai-student-collective-2026-2027-apply-to-become-a-campus-lead-and-receive-funding-chatgpt-access-codex-credits-and-a-cash-stipend/).
- Scale of student use on ChatGPT (OpenAI data report, 26 Aug 2026): US "prompts tied to classwork and homework peak at more than 460 million messages per week during the school year, with a measurable spike on Sunday evenings"; "as many as 70 million conversations per week" are people "testing their own knowledge" — [TUN summarising OpenAI's report](https://www.tun.com/home/openai-report-460m-weekly-student-messages-sent-to-chatgpt/) (secondary).
- Memory: launched April 2024; in 2026 OpenAI added "Dreaming" ("memories are automatically updated as time passes", e.g. a trip memory rewritten once the trip has happened; "Dreaming V3 ... 82.8 percent task success in factual recall") — [OpenAI, Dreaming: Better memory for a more helpful ChatGPT](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-memory-dreaming/) (via search summary); and "Memory Sources across all ChatGPT plans, giving users visibility into what context was used to personalize each response — what saved memories, past chats, or knowledge files influenced the answer" — [EdTech Innovation Hub](https://www.edtechinnovationhub.com/news/openai-rolls-out-new-chatgpt-memory-system-to-keep-personalization-current) and [Beginners in AI, What's new in ChatGPT 2026](https://beginnersinai.org/whats-new-chatgpt-2026/) (secondary).
- Projects: "each Project acting as a self-contained workspace with three layers: a system instruction set, uploaded files (5 to 40 depending on tier), and a Project Memory scope"; "Memories created in main chat do not flow into Projects, and Project memories do not leak into other Projects or main chat"; "Projects do not have built-in calendar features or collaboration features as of May 2026, with Shared Projects (multi-user) available only for Business and Enterprise tiers" — [Suprmind, ChatGPT Features 2026](https://suprmind.ai/hub/chatgpt/features/) (secondary; it dates the current Projects design to Nov 2025).
- Personal finance in ChatGPT (a structured, connected-data dashboard, i.e. the closest OpenAI has come to owning domain state inside chat): preview to US Pro users 15 May 2026, connect accounts via Plaid ("more than 12,000 financial institutions"), "a dashboard ... portfolio performance, spending, subscriptions, upcoming payments", users "can ask ChatGPT to remember finance-specific goals or obligations"; extended to Plus users 25 Jun 2026 on web, iOS, Android — [OpenAI, Personal finance in ChatGPT](https://openai.com/index/personal-finance-chatgpt/) (via search summary); [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/openai-new-personal-finance-experience-in-chatgpt-lets-pro-users-connect-financial-accounts-and-get-money-insights/); [Lead with AI, 19 May 2026](https://www.leadwithai.co/article/chatgpt-wants-to-help-manage-your-money).
- Apps SDK / App Directory: submissions opened 17 Dec 2025 and the directory launched 18 Dec 2025 for Plus/Team/Enterprise; apps reach Free/Plus/Pro/Go users from early 2026 (EEA, Switzerland, UK excluded initially); early partners include Booking.com, Canva, Coursera, Figma, Expedia, Spotify, Zillow — [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/openai-now-accepting-chatgpt-app-submissions-from-third-party-devs-launches); [Phiture, ChatGPT App Directory 2026](https://phiture.com/asostack/chat-gpt-app-directory/); [WebFX](https://www.webfx.com/blog/ai/chatgpt-apps/).

### Inferences
- Every OpenAI student feature operates on the student's *materials* (uploaded files, chat context) and on *free-text memory*. There is no typed academic object model anywhere in the consumer product; the one structured dashboard OpenAI has built (personal finance) shows it is willing to build domain dashboards when a data pipe (Plaid) exists, but nothing comparable exists for LMS/syllabus data as of Sept 2026.
- The Aug-2026 college-student add-on is gated behind institutional ChatGPT Edu deployments, not sold to individuals, so a direct-to-student planner is not competing with it on the same distribution channel.

### Gaps
- Could not fetch OpenAI's own text for Study Mode, the Aug-2026 education trio, or Projects; feature naming ("plugin") and the Nov-2025 Projects date come from secondary summaries and should be checked against [OpenAI's release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes).
- No source found stating whether Study Mode persists quiz results or a progress record across sessions beyond generic memory; treat "no progress tracking" as unconfirmed absence rather than confirmed.

## KQ2. What Anthropic has shipped for students (Claude for Education, learning mode, memory, connectors/directory, plugins, MCP Apps)

### Takeaway
Anthropic's student offering is Learning mode (Socratic response style), LMS/content hookups (Canvas LTI, Panopto and Wiley via MCP), institutional deals, and general-purpose memory that became free for everyone in March 2026. The most consequential 2025–26 moves for a connector-based planner are on the platform side: connectors on all plans (Apr 2026), in-chat "Suggested Connectors", MCP Apps (interactive UI in the chat), plugins (Sept 2026), scheduled/proactive work in the merged Cowork-chat (Sept 2026), and a directory with free, non-negotiated listing but no monetization rails.

Feb 2026 "AI Fluency Index" education report studied 9,830 Claude.ai conversations over a 7-day window in Jan 2026 — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/AI-fluency-index) (redirects to academy.claude.com, blocked; via search summary).

### Inferences
- Anthropic stores exactly two kinds of persistent user state relevant to a student: a free-text memory summary (global and per-project) and project files/instructions. There is no structured academic object model and the directory policy forbids third-party connectors from reading Claude's memory, so an external planner cannot even mine that state; the flow only runs the other way (the planner is the system of record, Claude reads/writes it via tools).
- The platform is actively pushing traffic to directory connectors (Suggested Connectors, usage-based ranking, all-plans availability, mobile beta, one discovery surface across chat/Code), which is favourable distribution for a connector-first planner, and MCP Apps mean a "briefing" can be rendered as a widget inside the chat rather than plain text.
- Anthropic's own education roadmap is institution- and teacher-facing (Canvas LTI, Claude for Teachers, university deals); nothing announced points at a consumer student planner.

### Gaps
- The Aug-2025 general-availability post for "learning modes" in Claude.ai/Claude Code could not be located (both anthropic.com/news/learning-modes and claude.com/blog/learning-modes return 404), so the exact date learning mode became available to all consumer users is unverified here.
- The primary text of the March-2026 memory-for-Free announcement was not found on claude.com/blog; the date rests on Dataconomy/TechBuzz.

## KQ3. What Google has shipped for students (Gemini Guided Learning, Gemini in Classroom, NotebookLM/Gemini Notebook, Learn Your Way, student offers)

### Takeaway
Google is the only platform whose stack touches structured academic state, but it does so through Google Classroom (the LMS holds classes, assignments and materials) with Gemini/NotebookLM reading that context: Guided Learning, class-tailored flashcards/quizzes, personal class notebooks, and Classroom-contextualised starter prompts (Aug 2026). There is still no grade projection, hours log, or cross-course semester plan in the AI layer, and NotebookLM's only persistent study state is flashcard "Got it / Missed it" sorting.

### Cited Findings
- Guided Learning in Gemini "is designed to slow students down and guide them through learning processes step by step, rather than simply providing direct answers" — [Pearson, Feb 2026](https://www.pearson.com/international-schools/international-schools-blog/2026/02/what-is-gemini_s-guided-learning-and-chatgpts-study-mode-.html). An RCT in Sierra Leone with 1,763 junior-secondary students over eight weeks found +0.258 SD in math — [Google DeepMind blog](https://deepmind.google/blog/measuring-the-impact-of-learning-with-ai-in-sierra-leone-and-beyond/) (via search summary).
- Gemini in Classroom expanded to K-12 and higher-ed students "of all ages" from 10 Aug 2026 (announced 4 Aug 2026): a Gemini tab in Classroom "to transform their class materials into interactive experiences ... such as creating flashcards or practice quizzes with Gemini tailored to their class"; students "with Gemini Notebook access can also sync materials provided by their teacher to Gemini Notebook"; "Starter prompts ... can now use context from Classroom", letting a student "select a specific class and assignment, with the Gemini prompt incorporating the relevant title, assignment instructions, and curriculum materials" — [Google Workspace Updates, 4 Aug 2026](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/08/gemini-in-google-classroom-is-expanding-to-users-of-all-ages-with-contextualized-Gemini-starter-prompts-for-students.html) (via search summary).
- NotebookLM in Classroom: "Students can now create personal class notebooks with NotebookLM in Google Classroom, with full rollout starting April 27, 2026" — [Google Workspace Updates, Apr 2026](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/04/students-can-now-create-personal-class-notebooks-with-NotebookLM-in-Google-Classroom.html) (via search summary).
- NotebookLM study features 2026: study guides; "flashcard decks remember your 'Got it / Missed it' sort between sessions" (2026 Studio update); Audio Overviews in 80+ languages by Mar 2026; free tier 50 sources/notebook; Drive auto-sync added at I/O 2026 — [Jeff Su, NotebookLM in 2026](https://www.jeffsu.org/notebooklm-changed-completely-heres-what-matters-in-2026/), [AI Native Student, NotebookLM for studying 2026](https://ainativestudent.com/blog/how-to-use-notebooklm-for-studying-2026/) (secondary). The product is being referred to as "Gemini Notebook" in 2026 — [Glasp](https://glasp.co/articles/notebooklm-2026) and the Aug-2026 Workspace post above.
- ISTE 2026 student announcements (June 2026): "Learn with Gemini" lets students "get guided help or create study guides, practice quizzes, and flashcards – all based on their class curriculum" — [Google blog, ISTE 2026 students](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/education/iste-students-2026/) (via search summary).
- Learn Your Way (research experiment, released 16 Sep 2025): turns a textbook/PDF into "narrated slides, audio lessons, quizzes and mind maps" adapted to grade and interests; powered by LearnLM; in a 60-student Chicago trial users "scored 9% higher on immediate tests and retained 11% more knowledge after a few days" — [Google blog, Learn Your Way](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/education/learn-your-way/) (via search summary), [Maginative](https://www.maginative.com/article/learn-your-way-googles-new-ai-transforms-static-textbooks-into-interactive-personalized-lessons/), paper [arXiv 2509.13348](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.13348v4).
- Gemini for Students landing page offers a free student plan ("your AI study buddy") — [gemini.google/students](https://gemini.google/students/) (blocked; existence via search); 2026 terms summarised by secondary sites [Mexta](https://www.mextalearn.com/blog/gemini-for-students-2026) and [TaxHeal](https://www.taxheal.com/google-gemini-back-to-school-2026.html) (not verified).

### Inferences
- Google's advantage is the LMS: Classroom already stores classes, assignments, due dates and teacher-posted materials, so Gemini gets structured context for free. But this only exists where a school runs Classroom and an admin grants access; it is teacher-authored state, not the student's own plan, and none of it is gradebook-derived projection or time tracking.
- For a university student whose institution uses Canvas/Brightspace/Moodle rather than Classroom, Google's structured advantage mostly evaporates and NotebookLM becomes a materials-and-flashcards tool like the rest.

### Gaps
- Could not verify the exact 2026 Gemini student offer (duration, eligibility, which plan) from Google's page; the Aug-2025 launch date of Guided Learning is not confirmed by any fetched 2026 source.
- No Google source found announcing any planner, deadline ledger, grade projection or hours-tracking feature in Gemini or NotebookLM.

## KQ4. Does any platform hold persistent, structured academic state (term, courses, deadlines, grades, hours)? Any announced plans?

### Takeaway
No. As of Sept 2026 the three platforms hold only free-text memory, uploaded files/instructions (Projects/notebooks), and (Google only) LMS-sourced context from Classroom. No platform stores a typed semester model, does weighted-grade math, or logs study hours, and no announcement found points that way; the nearest analogue is OpenAI's May-2026 personal-finance dashboard, which proves the pattern (connect a data pipe → structured dashboard in chat) but for a different domain.

### Cited Findings
- OpenAI memory is a running set of free-text memories, revised over time by "Dreaming" — [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-memory-dreaming/) (via search summary); Projects hold "a system instruction set, uploaded files ... and a Project Memory scope" with "no built-in calendar features" as of May 2026 — [Suprmind](https://suprmind.ai/hub/chatgpt/features/) (secondary).
- Study Mode personalisation comes from "stored memories from previous chats and questions that allow ChatGPT to assess your abilities" — [Appscribed](https://appscribed.com/chatgpt-study-mode/) (secondary, paraphrasing the OpenAI FAQ).
- OpenAI's personal-finance experience stores connected-account data and shows "spending, subscriptions, upcoming payments"; users "can ask ChatGPT to remember finance-specific goals or obligations" — [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/personal-finance-chatgpt/) (via search summary).
- Claude memory is "a memory summary" the user can view and edit, scoped per project — [Claude blog, Memory](https://claude.com/blog/memory) (fetched); the redesigned Projects have "No explicit mention of built-in task management, deadlines, or structured data fields" — [Claude blog, Projects redesigned](https://claude.com/blog/projects-redesigned) (fetched); the Claude for Education pages carry "no information about ... grade tracking, deadline monitoring, or study time analytics" — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/introducing-claude-for-education), [claude.com/solutions/education](https://claude.com/solutions/education) (fetched).
- Third-party connectors "must not" query "Claude's memory, chat history, conversation summaries, or user files" — [Connector pre-submission checklist](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/review-criteria) (fetched); a memory vendor notes "connectors are not a memory system by themselves—they surface state from other systems" — [Mem0 blog](https://mem0.ai/blog/adding-memory-to-claude-connectors).
- Google: Gemini in Classroom pulls "the relevant title, assignment instructions, and curriculum materials as context directly from Google Classroom" — [Workspace Updates, Aug 2026](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/08/gemini-in-google-classroom-is-expanding-to-users-of-all-ages-with-contextualized-Gemini-starter-prompts-for-students.html) (via search summary); NotebookLM persists flashcard sort state only — [Jeff Su](https://www.jeffsu.org/notebooklm-changed-completely-heres-what-matters-in-2026/) (secondary).
- Coursework is a shrinking share of general-assistant traffic on Claude (19%→12%, Nov 2025→Feb 2026) — [Anthropic Economic Index](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-march-2026-report) (fetched) — while OpenAI reports 460M+ weekly US classwork messages — [TUN](https://www.tun.com/home/openai-report-460m-weekly-student-messages-sent-to-chatgpt/) (secondary).

### Inferences
- "Study mode + memory" gives a platform *pedagogy* and *personal context*, not a *ledger*. Grade projection, weighted schemes, hours per course and spaced-recall schedules require typed, mutable records with arithmetic over them; nothing in the platforms' memory designs (summaries rewritten by the model) is a reliable store for that, and Anthropic explicitly walls memory off from connectors.

The academic equivalent would be an LMS pipe (Canvas/Brightspace APIs) plus a dashboard; Google already has half of that via Classroom. No such move by OpenAI or Anthropic was found.

### Gaps
- No public roadmap statement from any of the three about a student planner, LMS-synced dashboard, or grade tracking was found; absence of evidence, not evidence of absence.

## KQ7. The "wrapper" risk: platform features that killed small AI apps, and small apps that survived by owning domain state or workflow

### Takeaway
The clean kill cases are thin prosumer wrappers with no data of their own (Jasper/Copy.ai after ChatGPT; slide generators after Copilot; niche chatbots after custom GPTs). Survival cases in the evidence are apps that either moved up-market with workflow (Jasper → enterprise) or kept compounding on a vertical asset while the platforms shipped adjacent features (Turbo AI's growth through 2025; Quizlet buying Coconote). OpenAI's May-2026 finance dashboard is the freshest example of the platform absorbing a "connect your data, see a dashboard" product.

### Cited Findings
- Jasper reached a $1.5B valuation on "a beautiful, simple interface on top of GPT-3"; after ChatGPT (Nov 2022) "the floor fell out" as users replaced "$50/month subscriptions ... with free and $20/mo premium subscriptions to ChatGPT"; Jasper "cut its internal valuation by 20%", laid off staff and cut 2023 revenue projections "up to 30%"; it then "niched down and went enterprise", projected near $90M revenue with 20%+ of the Fortune 500 — [Sacra, AI writing goes enterprise](https://sacra.com/research/copy-ai-jasper-ai-writing-tools), [Ben's Bites](https://bensbites.beehiiv.com/p/jasper-cuts-internal-valuation-growth-slows), [Turing Post](https://turingpost.substack.com/p/jasper-ai-a-dilemma-of-a-thin-wrapper).
- Tome (AI slide generator) vs Microsoft Copilot in PowerPoint: "they don't own the platform they're disrupting" — [DEV Community, The Graveyard of AI Startups](https://dev.to/dev_tips/the-graveyard-of-ai-startups-startups-that-forgot-to-build-real-value-5ad9) (opinion piece).
- A VC essay claims OpenAI's cadence "(GPT Store, Operator, Tasks, Canvas, native file upload) directly cannibalized more than 200 funded 'GPT wrapper' startups in 2024, and its May 2026 personal-finance feature inside ChatGPT undercut a wave of AI expense-tracking apps overnight", and that ~80% of wrapper startups will fail by end-2026 — [Value Add VC](https://valueaddvc.com/blog/how-to-build-a-startup-in-a-market-where-ai-will-eventually-do-what-you-do) (unverified opinion; the "200" and "80%" figures have no cited methodology).

- "Sherlocking" defined as a platform absorbing "a third-party startup's core feature directly into its own product, usually for free or as part of an existing subscription" — [Value Add VC, Features not companies](https://valueaddvc.com/blog/why-most-ai-startups-are-building-features-not-companies); running graveyards at [Killed by LLM](https://killedbyllm.com/) and [Killed by AI](https://mixtpatrik.github.io/killedbyai/).
- Counter-evidence (vertical study apps kept growing after NotebookLM and Study Mode shipped): Turbo AI hit 5M users and an eight-figure run rate by Oct 2025, profitable on $750K raised — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/23/20-year-old-dropouts-built-ai-notetaker-turbo-ai-to-5-million-users); Quizlet acquired Coconote in Feb 2026 rather than being displaced — [THE Journal](https://thejournal.com/articles/2026/02/13/quizlet-adds-ai-powered-tools-for-active-practice-notetaking.aspx); Knowunity raised $31M in Jun 2025 — [Seedtable](https://seedtable.com/companies/knowunity/funding-rounds/series-b-2025-06).
- Anthropic's directory terms reserve the right to remove any listing "at any time for any reason ... without liability to you" and to "collect and use functional metadata about your Software to improve the Anthropic Services" (terms last updated 16 Mar 2026) — [Anthropic Software Directory Terms](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13145338-anthropic-software-directory-terms) (fetched).

### Inferences
- The apps that died sold the model's output (copy, slides, chat) with no proprietary state; the platforms could replicate them with a prompt. A planner's value is a mutable ledger (weights, due dates, hours, recall history) plus arithmetic and scheduling logic over it; the platforms' memory is a summary rewritten by the model, which is the wrong primitive for that, and Anthropic's policy stops connectors reading it anyway.
- The realistic threat is not "study mode + memory" but "LMS connector + dashboard" (the finance pattern). Google is closest (Classroom context in Gemini, Aug 2026). For OpenAI/Anthropic it would require Canvas/Brightspace integrations at consumer scale, which as of Sept 2026 exist only as institutional LTI hookups (Claude for Education) or Edu-deployment add-ons (ChatGPT).
- The directory terms make platform dependence explicit: listing is at Anthropic's discretion and usage metadata flows to Anthropic. A planner that is also a standalone web/mobile app with its own login and data is protected against delisting in a way a chat-only app is not.

### Gaps
- No documented case found of an *education* app specifically killed by Study Mode, Guided Learning or NotebookLM; the study-app category's 2025–26 evidence points the other way (growth and consolidation).
- The "200 startups cannibalized" and "80% will fail" figures are unsourced; treat as rhetoric.

## KQ9. Sober read: where a planner-with-connector is exposed vs. protected (synthesis of the above)

### Takeaway
Exposed on everything that is a transformation of the student's own documents (summaries, flashcards, quizzes, Socratic tutoring) and on generic memory ("remember my exam is Friday"). Protected on the ledger: a typed semester (courses, weighted assessments, grading scheme, pages, hours, recall history) with arithmetic and scheduling over it, kept in a system the platforms neither store nor let connectors read, and reachable from the chat the student already lives in via a distribution channel that is currently free, all-plan, and actively suggested in-chat.

### Cited Findings
- Platform tutoring/recall features exist on all three: Study Mode quizzes — [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-study-mode/); Gemini class-tailored flashcards and quizzes — [Workspace Updates, Aug 2026](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/08/gemini-in-google-classroom-is-expanding-to-users-of-all-ages-with-contextualized-Gemini-starter-prompts-for-students.html); NotebookLM flashcards with persisted sort — [Jeff Su](https://www.jeffsu.org/notebooklm-changed-completely-heres-what-matters-in-2026/); Learning mode — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/introducing-claude-for-education).
- No platform stores structured academic state or grade math (see KQ4 citations); connectors are forbidden from reading Claude memory — [Review criteria](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/review-criteria).
- Claude connectors are on all plans with in-chat suggestion and usage ranking — [Everyday-life connectors](https://claude.com/blog/connectors-for-everyday-life), [Directory](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/directory); scheduled/proactive work now exists in Claude — [Cowork is now Claude](https://claude.com/blog/cowork-is-now-claude); MCP Apps allow inline UI — [MCP Apps](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/mcp-apps/getting-started).
- OpenAI's finance dashboard shows the platform's appetite for connected-data dashboards — [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/personal-finance-chatgpt/); Google already injects Classroom assignment context into Gemini — [Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/08/gemini-in-google-classroom-is-expanding-to-users-of-all-ages-with-contextualized-Gemini-starter-prompts-for-students.html).
- Half of students want education-specific tools and worry about accuracy of general tools — [Chegg 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250128439546/en/Chegg-Global-Student-Survey-2025-80-of-Undergraduates-Worldwide-Have-Used-GenAI-to-Support-their-Studies-But-Accuracy-a-Top-Concern); planning is absent from every task list surveyed (KQ6).
- Anthropic can delist at will and collects usage metadata — [Directory Terms](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13145338-anthropic-software-directory-terms).

### Inferences
- Exposed surface 1 — recall quizzes and "explain this": Akada's recall-quiz tool competes head-on with Study Mode, Guided Learning and NotebookLM; its only defensible angle is that quiz results write back into a per-course recall schedule the platforms do not keep.
- Exposed surface 2 — "remember my deadlines": platform memory can hold a handful of dates as text; it cannot compute "what grade do I need on the final", weekly hours per course, or a spaced schedule, and it is periodically rewritten by the model (OpenAI "Dreaming"). Akada should never position on "remembers your deadlines" and always on the arithmetic and the ledger.
- Exposed surface 3 — the input path: "attach a syllabus and say put it in Akada" is a prompt-and-tools flow any platform could replicate if it had somewhere to put the typed tasks. Today it has nowhere; the thing to watch is a first-party LMS connector plus dashboard (finance pattern), most likely from Google via Classroom, less likely from OpenAI/Anthropic before an institutional LMS pipe becomes consumer-grade.
- Protected surface — the system of record: typed exams with weights, readings with pages, grading-scheme proposals, session logs, and the "briefing" are all derived from state that lives in Akada's database, which is exactly the thing the surviving wrappers (Jasper→enterprise workflow; Turbo/Quizlet vertical assets) had and the dead ones did not. Owning a standalone app with its own login also neutralises the delisting and metadata clauses.
- Distribution read: Claude's rails are the cheapest app-store-like channel available to a solo founder in Sept 2026 (free, no partner deal, all plans, in-chat suggestion), but they carry no billing and no guaranteed placement; OpenAI's directory is larger but forbade digital subscriptions at launch and pushes external checkout. Both point to the same posture: sell the subscription on Akada's own site, treat the connectors as free acquisition and daily-use surfaces, and keep the MCP server portable across both.
- Category economics: the study-app market clears at $5–$20/month with Turbo AI proving a bootstrapped path to 5M users; a planner priced inside that band with a semester-long retention loop (deadlines and grades change weekly) has a plausible wedge, provided the input friction is lower than Shovel/DormWay's syllabus upload, which the Claude connector arguably achieves.

### Gaps
- No direct evidence on retention or willingness-to-pay for AI-connected planners specifically (Shovel/DormWay publish no numbers).
- No evidence either way on whether Anthropic or OpenAI plan consumer LMS integrations; this is the single biggest unknown for the "protected" thesis.
